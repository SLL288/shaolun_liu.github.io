About

I am an independent researcher and engineer working at the intersection of software security, CI/CD, and geospatial analysis. My current work includes a schema-aware approach to API fuzzing (PrediQL) and robust geospatial workflows (QGIS, WhiteboxTools) for mapping and analysis.
