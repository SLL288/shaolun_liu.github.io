About

I am an independent researcher and engineer working at the intersection of software security, machine learning, and geospatial analysis. My current work includes a schema-aware, multi-armed-bandit approach to GraphQL fuzzing (PrediQL) and field-informed geospatial workflows (QGIS, WhiteboxTools) to identify paleo-channels and placer gold targets in Liberia.

Previously, I have collaborated with the Cybersecurity Lab of Mohammad A. Tayeb on security testing research. I enjoy building complete pipelines: data collection → modeling → evaluation → deployment.

Research Interests

GraphQL Security Testing

LLM-assisted, schema-aware fuzzing; dependency reasoning between queries/mutations; multi-armed bandit strategy selection; negative coverage metrics.

Geospatial Analytics for Placer Mining

DEM-derived hydrology (HAND, TPI, curvature), paleo-channel detection, UAV/remote sensing workflows, stream hotspot ranking.

LLM Systems & RAG

Local LLMs (Ollama), FAISS embeddings, schema/document flattening, safety & evaluation, reproducible pipelines.

Practical Software Engineering

End-to-end Python toolchains, Bash automation, data engineering for scientific computing and field operations.

Selected Projects

PrediQL — Schema-Aware GraphQL Fuzzing

Automatic generation of valid GraphQL queries/mutations with adaptive exploration; integrates GraphQler outputs, LLM mutation, and coverage tracking. Status: research prototype.

Highlights: multi-armed bandit for strategy selection; schema dependency inference; realistic parameter injection.

Paleo-Channel Ranking Pipeline

QGIS + WhiteboxTools processing chain for Liberia DEM tiles: stream extraction, HAND filters, TPI bands, paleo-score compositing, hotspot vectorization for field sampling.
